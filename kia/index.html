Chapter one

The night it started

September 6–7

It started at Senayan. Neither of us called it a date that night. A couple of weeks later I did, in a text, very casually, as if I hadn’t been thinking about it.

At 00:38 I sent the most original opening line in history.

hai manis

00:38hi, sweet

lemme know when u get home pruy

02:15

already

02:36

thanks kia

02:40

That was the first time she called me pruy. I still don’t know what it means. I’ve stopped asking.

she asked me to text when I got home. that’s how it starts with her: making sure you got home.

Chapter two

Eleven days of nothing

September 7–18

Then nothing, for eleven days. Not my proudest statistic.

On the 18th, just after midnight, I came back with a strategy: send “p”. Then “seng”. Then “pls”. Then “syg”. Then “manis”. All within thirty seconds.

sorry ini siapa ya?

00:36sorry, who’s this?

WKWKWKWK ANJ

00:36(laughing, wounded)

Fair. Also, that was the first “seng” in the chat. It has since been used 514 times, by both of us, for everything.

Chapter three

Things got loud

September 22–23

September 22 had 1,059 messages. It was the first day the chat got loud, and it never really got quiet again.

It was also the day she first called me honey, and the day of the first “i love u” in this chat. For the record, it was hers. Half past midnight, said like it was nothing:

yang penting i love u

Sep 22 · 00:30what matters is, i love u

i just feel i do love u

Sep 23 · 02:32

My contribution that week, translated: “the more we meet, the more I miss you.” I added “bro” at the end so it wouldn’t sound too serious. It sounded serious anyway.

Chapter five

Find My, Roblox & LSPR

September 25 – October 1

She found out where I was before I told her. Find My comes up 28 times in this chat. I have never once successfully pretended to be somewhere else.

On September 30 we had our longest call: one full hour. Somewhere around then I asked if she wanted to play Roblox. She said, and I quote, “ngga main game at all” (I don’t play games at all). I took it well.

The next day she told me she was looking at colleges.

PLIS SUPPORT GUEE

Oct 1 · 20:24please support me

lspr next year

20:24

Supported. Still supported. I’ll keep saying it until she’s sick of it.

Chapter six

October, mostly Blok M

October 2–18

October was MRT rides, late coffee, Senayan, Bintaro, and Blok M again. A lot of it looked like this:

  • “pulang jam berapa?” what time are you going home? Asked by her, 18 times.
  • “udah sampe” I’m home. Reported by her, 21 times.
  • “makan apa?” what are you eating? Food came up 122 times.
  • coffee 42 mentions, most of them after 11 pm, which she has opinions about.

Nothing dramatic. Just two people checking that the other one ate and got home. Looking back, that was most of it, and it was enough.

Chapter seven

The 2,034-message day

October 19–22

Four days in a row with more than a thousand messages each. Then October 22: 2,034, the busiest day in this whole chat.

Most of it was a game of questions that went from midnight to 3 am. Somewhere in the middle I typed “u kno me so wel kn”, spelling not included. She did.

Two nights before that, she sent this:

mau sama km itu ofc tapi mimpi juga bisa ketemu km

Oct 20 · 01:21of course I want to be with you, but I can also see you in my dreams

i love you so much qil

Oct 20 · 01:24

i always know right where i am honeyy

Oct 21 · 01:41
I read the dream one twice.

Chapter eight

Small things

October 28 – November 4

Some facts I learned about her this month, in no particular order:

  • She will not show you her ID photo. It’s from high school. She said she’d rather have her hair pulled.
  • She is, by her own appointment, a coffee judge. “juri kopi, seng.” I didn’t know it was a job either.
  • 😭 is not for crying. It’s for laughing, being shy, being annoyed, and missing someone. She used it 453 times.
  • She asks if you’ve eaten before almost anything else. Almost every time.

Chapter nine

Not every night was easy

November 1

This book would be lying if every chapter was nice. On November 1 I went to a party and forgot to say so. She thought I was on my way home, and she waited up until 3 am.

aku tungguin sampe jam 3

08:47I waited up until 3

For the record: in this chat I said sorry 74 times. She said it 19 times. I’m not proud of the ratio, but I’m glad she kept answering.

lesson learned: say “party” before, not after.

Chapter ten

Main characters

November 5

At two minutes past midnight, out of nowhere:

aku kangen kamu deh

00:02I kind of miss you

kangen yang kangen apa kangen biasa

00:02miss-miss, or regular miss?

Two and a half hours later, she settled it:

we are the main characters ngl

02:34

I don’t usually agree with things said at 2:34 am. I agreed with that one.

Chapter eleven

To be continued

November 8 – today

She’s sorting out college now. Last night she went wandering around PIK Avenue and I asked a lot of questions about why PIK.

That’s where the chat is today. 57 days, 208 calls, about fourteen and a half hours on the phone, and more than half of all our messages sent between midnight and 5 am.

I’m not good at saying this kind of thing out loud, which is why it’s a website. So, short version: thanks for asking if I got home. Thanks for the 😭. I like this story, and I’d like to keep reading it.

— Shaquille (pruy)
